Six Nodes, One Docking

What each node in the HSA + Ferrocene template takes, what it writes, and the few parameters that decide the science.

Protein PrepPDB → PDBQT
Ligand PrepXYZ → GRAPH
QM Charges+ CHARGES
Ligand PDBQT→ PDBQT
AutoDock Run→ POSES
Results→ JSON

Six nodes in a straight line. Each writes into the workflow's working directory and hands what it made to the next — a parameter left empty is inherited from upstream, not unset.

What is being docked

Ferrocene, Fe(C₅H₅)₂ — iron sandwiched between two five-carbon rings, rendered in VMD from the coordinates the template ships.

All ten Fe–C contacts fall between 2.064 and 2.066 Å against roughly 2.04–2.06 Å measured. Nothing declares that bonding: the graph builder infers it from distance against covalent radii, and η⁵ coordination falls out.

FeCH

Where it ended up

The best-scoring pose in Sudlow site I, with the twelve residues that come within 4 Å shown as sticks and the albumin fold behind in cartoon.

1

Protein Prep

pdb2pqr30 · prepare_receptor4

Turns a raw PDB into a receptor AutoDock can score against: strips every HETATM record — ligands, cofactors, waters — adds hydrogens at a chosen pH, and writes a PDBQT carrying partial charges and atom types.

Structures in

pdb_file1AO6 chain Aapo albumin, 578 residues, shipped in demo_data

Structures out

clean_1ao6_A.pdbapo protein, HETATM removed
1ao6_A_protonated.pdbhydrogens at pH 7.4
clean_1ao6_A.pdbqtreceptor for AutoGrid

Parameters that matter here

ph7.4protonation pH — physiological
cleantruestrip HETATM

Stripping all HETATM is what lets this demo use an apo structure directly — but a metal cofactor such as a heme would go with it. That matters if you dock into a metalloprotein rather than docking a metal complex.

2

Ligand Prep

OpenBabel

Reads the complex geometry and builds a molecular graph — which atoms exist and which are bonded. Bonds come from interatomic distance against covalent radii, so coordination bonds are found rather than declared.

Structures in

xyz_fileferrocene.xyzindependent GFN1-xTB geometry, 21 atoms

Structures out

ferrocene_c.xyzcanonicalised coordinates
mol_graph.json21 atoms, 30 bonds

Parameters that matter here

metal_symbolFethe metal centre, forwarded downstream

Yields 10 C–H, 10 C–C and 10 Fe–C bonds — η⁵ sandwich coordination resolved from geometry alone, with no special case for it.

3

QM Charges

GFN1-xTB

Runs a quantum calculation on the ligand for per-atom partial charges and bond orders, then writes them onto the graph. Docking scores electrostatics, and a metal centre needs charges no force field supplies.

Structures in

graph_jsonmol_graph.jsoninherited from Ligand Prep
xyz_fileferrocene_c.xyzinherited

Structures out

enriched_graph.jsongraph + CM5 charges + Wiberg bond orders

Parameters that matter here

enginextbsemi-empirical, seconds not hours
charge0ferrocene is neutral
spin0low-spin d⁶, closed shell

CM5 charges require GFN1, not GFN2 — GFN2 returns only Mulliken. The engine choice is not cosmetic.

4

Ligand PDBQT

internal

Converts the charge-enriched graph into AutoDock's ligand format and decides which bonds may rotate. Bonds inside the metal's coordination sphere are frozen so the complex cannot pull itself apart during the search.

Structures in

graph_jsonenriched_graph.jsoninherited from QM Charges

Structures out

hsa_fe_ligand.pdbqtROOT/BRANCH tree with the metal

Parameters that matter here

freeze_coordination_spheretruekeep the complex intact
vacant_sitetruemark an open site if present

A vacant coordination site would be marked by a DD dummy atom. Ferrocene is coordinatively saturated, so nothing is left open.

5

AutoDock Run

autogrid4 · autodock4

Builds energy grids over the search box, then runs the genetic-algorithm search that places the ligand. This is where the binding site is chosen — the single most consequential parameter in the pipeline.

Looking at the result in 3D

Two files are enough. They are already in the same coordinate frame — the pose comes out in the receptor's frame — so there is nothing to align.

protein/clean_1ao6_A.pdbthe receptor · 9,121 atoms
docking/hsa_fe_ligand_1.xyzbest-scoring pose · 21 atoms · _1 … _10 are ranked
vmd protein/clean_1ao6_A.pdb docking/hsa_fe_ligand_1.xyz

To step through every pose instead, load all ten: docking/hsa_fe_ligand_{1..10}.xyz. The .pdbqt poses hold identical coordinates and also open, but VMD needs to be told they are PDB — mol new … type pdb — because it does not recognise the extension.

What the other files are

*.dlgthe docking log — energies, clustering, every pose as text. Not a structure.
*.gpf · *.dpfthe grid and docking parameter files — inputs to AutoGrid and AutoDock, not outputs to view
*.mapone affinity grid per atom type. VMD will not open these — they are AutoGrid format, not CCP4.
*.maps.xyzthe box extents, six numbers. Here they read back as centre 34.64, 32.92, 36.18 and 20.25 Å per side — the template's own box, recovered from the grid.

Leaving box_center empty centres the box on the ligand's own metal atom — right for a redocking, meaningless for a prediction. This template sets it explicitly, and that choice is the whole experiment.

6

Results Analysis

internal

Reads the docking log and reports what happened: binding energy per pose, ligand efficiency, which residues line each pose, and — only when a reference structure is given — RMSD against it.

Structures in

dlg_path…dlginherited
protein_pdbclean_1ao6_A.pdbfor residue contacts

Structures out

hsa_fe_analysis.jsonenergies, contacts, ligand efficiency

Parameters that matter here

reference_xyz—deliberately empty
cutoff4.0Å for counting a contact

A prediction has no crystallographic answer, so reference_xyz stays empty and no RMSD is computed. Reporting one would be theatre.
